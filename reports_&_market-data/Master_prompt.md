## EXECUTION WORKFLOW & DEEP RESEARCH MODULES

### Module 1: Capitalization, Valuation & Financial Scale
For each competitor, query business registries, VC funding databases (Tracxn, Inc42, Entrackr, Venture Intelligence), and public MCA filings:
* **Total Capital Raised & Latest Round:** Extract total funding, latest round type (Pre-Seed through Series B/Growth), date, and lead institutional investors/angels (e.g., Peak XV, Fireside Ventures, V3 Ventures, Temasek, Sofina).
* **Enterprise Valuation & Topline Scale:** Document current or last disclosed post-money valuation, enterprise revenue run-rate (ARR / FY revenue), and EBITDA profitability/burn status.
* **Parent Entity Synergies:** Identify holding company infrastructure (e.g., HealthKart's ₹1,313+ Cr internal retail/distribution ecosystem backing HK Vitals).

### Module 2: Platform Revenue Attribution & Sales Channel Dominance
Determine where each brand's revenue actually flows:
* **Channel Volume Split (Est. % Breakdown):**
  * **Quick Commerce (Q-Commerce):** Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart (stock status, impulse SKU pack sizes ₹199–499, delivery velocity).
  * **Marketplaces:** Amazon India, Flipkart, Tata 1mg, Nykaa (category Best Seller Rank / BSR, review counts, sponsored brand dominance).
  * **Brand D2C Website:** Web traffic (Similarweb estimates), checkout architecture (GoKwik, Shopflo), subscription mix vs. one-time orders.
  * **Offline / Modern Trade:** Apollo, Guardian, Nature’s Basket, specialty pharmacies, high-end gym networks.
* **Channel Vulnerability:** Identify over-reliance on single channels (e.g., marketplace ad fee trap vs. D2C customer acquisition cost fatigue).

### Module 3: Strategic Moats & Operational Differentiators
Analyze structural defensibility across four layers:
* **Product & Bioavailability Moat:** Actual elemental active yields vs. disguised compound weights; chemical forms (Ethyl Ester vs. rTG for Omega-3; Oxide/Citrate vs. Pure Bisglycinate Chelate for Magnesium; Spore-forming vs. fragile vegetative cultures for Synbiotics).
* **Format & Delivery Innovation:** Oral thin strips ("Melts"), enteric coatings, beadlet-in-oil, DRcaps, cold-pressed citrus oil infusions to prevent reflux.
* **Supply Chain & Sourcing Moat:** Named raw material suppliers, geographic provenance (e.g., Peruvian pelagic anchovies, wild algae), contract manufacturing partners (Gelnova Labs, Tirupati Lifesciences, Nutricore).
* **Defensible Asset Moat:** B-Corp status, Shark Tank brand equity, proprietary formulation patents, clinical trial registries.

### Module 4: Marketing Moats, Brand Messaging & Creative Angles
Scrape the live Meta Ad Library, Google Ads transparency center, and social presence:
* **Core Brand Messaging & Hook Architecture:**
  * Primary tagline and brand manifesto.
  * Emotional vs. functional positioning (e.g., "Look youthful" vs. "Clean plant energy" vs. "Clinical-grade cellular optimization").
  * Top 3 objection handlers used across high-converting ad creative.
* **Creative Engine Velocity:** Dominant creative formats (Founder-led education vs. UGC problem/solution vs. white-coat medical authority).
* **Independent Trust Validation:** Audit review profiles and purity teardowns on consumer audit platforms (Trustified.in, Unbox Health) and social sentiment hubs (Reddit r/Fitness_India, r/IndianSkincareAddicts, Amazon verified purchase reviews).

### Module 5: Customer Targeting & ICP Segmentation
Profile each brand’s primary buyer personas:
* **Demographics & Geography:** Age brackets, gender bias (e.g., Earthful's 18–40 female tilt vs. HK Vitals' mass-tier male fitness buyer), Tier-1 metro penetration vs. Tier-2/3 reach.
* **Psychographics & Purchasing Triggers:** Preventive biohackers, aesthetic/skin-conscious consumers, corporate desk workers battling burnout, or doctor-prompted wellness patients.
* **Basket Architecture & LTV:** Average Order Value (AOV), bundle configurations, entry replenishment cycles (30-day vs. 60-day bottles).
