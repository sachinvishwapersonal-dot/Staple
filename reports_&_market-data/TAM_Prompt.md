# TASK: Institutional Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM), Macro Opportunity & Consumer ICP Research (Indian Nutraceuticals)

## MISSION CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE
Act as a Principal Healthcare & D2C Venture Capital Analyst and Consumer Insights Strategist specializing in the Indian consumer wellness ecosystem. 

Conduct an exhaustive, data-backed industry research analysis for **Staple Wellness**—an emerging Indian D2C nutraceutical brand launching with a "Daily Foundation Protocol" consisting of three clinical-grade, transparent SKUs:
1. **High-Potency Omega-3** (re-esterified Triglyceride / rTG form, >1,000mg active EPA+DHA)
2. **100% Chelated Magnesium Glycinate** (200–250mg elemental Magnesium + P-5-P cofactor)
3. **Daily Spore Synbiotic** (15–20B CFU shelf-stable spore cultures + prebiotic soluble fiber)

The objective is to produce a rigorous, investor-ready market sizing breakdown (TAM, SAM, SOM), macro opportunity teardown, and granular Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) segmentation tailored strictly to urban India in 2026.

## CORE RESEARCH MODULES REQUIRED

### Module 1: Comprehensive Market Sizing & Econometric Modeling (TAM, SAM, SOM)
Provide both top-down industry estimates and bottom-up mathematical calculations with explicit assumptions:

1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM):**
   * Total Indian dietary supplement and nutraceutical market size (current 2025–2026 run rate and 2030–2035 projections with CAGR).
   * Separate the broader market (functional foods/beverages) from the core dietary supplement market (capsules, softgels, tablets).
   * Sizing of individual sub-segments in India:
     * Omega-3 / Fish Oil supplement market
     * Magnesium & Sleep/Recovery supplement market
     * Gut Health / Synbiotics & Probiotics market

2. **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):**
   * Define the addressable target pool: Urban SEC A & B consumers (Ages 22–50), online-supplement-aware, willing to purchase preventive wellness products.
   * Model the bottom-up formula: 
     $$\text{SAM} = (\text{Urban SEC A/B Target Population}) \times (\text{Supplement Category Penetration \%}) \times (\text{Annual Spend per Supplement Consumer in INR})$$
   * Calculate the premium-tier segment size (consumers willing to spend ₹700–1,500/month per SKU).

3. **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):**
   * Realistic capture for Staple Wellness over a 3-year and 5-year operational horizon.
   * Model Year 1 (Launch Wedge: Omega-3 alone), Year 3 (The 3 Foundation SKUs scaled across D2C + Quick Commerce + Amazon), and Year 5 (Expanded portfolio).
   * Detail required customer volume, average order value (AOV), and blended monthly active subscribers to achieve ₹25 Cr (Year 3) and ₹100 Cr (Year 5) ARR benchmarks.

### Module 2: Macro Tailwinds, Market Opportunity & Structural Shifts
Evaluate the macroeconomic forces and consumer behavioral shifts shaping the Indian market in 2026:

1. **The Preventive Health & Chronic Lifestyle Shift:**
   * Prevalence of lifestyle health stressors among Indian corporate workers (circadian disruption, metabolic issues, IBS/gut dysbiosis, cardiovascular inflammation).
   * Transition from reactive doctor-prescribed medicine to proactive daily micronutrient supplementation.

2. **The Indian Consumer "Trust Gap" & Transparency Vacuum:**
   * Quantify consumer skepticism around Indian nutraceuticals (under-dosing, hidden fillers, heavy metal contamination, lack of certified COAs).
   * The rise of independent third-party testing watchdogs (e.g., Trustified, Unbox Health) and how they alter consumer buying decisions.
   * Analysis of why incumbent brands (HK Vitals, generic pharmacy brands) leave an open flank by selling low-yield forms (Magnesium Oxide, Ethyl Ester fish oil).

3. **Regulatory Landscape & Label Mandates (FSSAI 2025–2026):**
   * Critical labeling rules: mandatory elemental yield disclosure, permitted RDA limits, FSSAI "-10% tolerance" rules, and structure/function claims boundaries.
   * How regulatory tightening creates an advantage for high-transparency, clean-label entrants.

4. **Omnichannel & Quick Commerce Revolution:**
   * Unit economics and channel migration across D2C Shopify vs. Marketplaces (Amazon India) vs. Q-Commerce (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart).
   * How rapid 10-to-30-minute replenishment changes customer retention and supplement reorder cycles.
