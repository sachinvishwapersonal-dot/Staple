## Key Findings at a Glance

### The Competitive Landscape

| Brand | Funding | FY25 Revenue | Biggest Moat | Biggest Vulnerability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HK Vitals** | $349–382M | ₹1,313 Cr (parent) | ₹1,300 Cr ecosystem + 100 stores | Low potency (300mg Omega-3); Ethyl Ester form |
| **Wellbeing Nutrition** | $14–25.6M | ₹170 Cr | Melts® strip tech + USV pharma backing | Still -₹30 Cr loss; format limits dosing |
| **Earthful** | ~$4M | ₹15–30 Cr (est.) | Clean-label + Shark Tank + women's health | "Natural" limits potency claims; limited capital |
| **Ace Blend** | $4.48M | ₹12–40 Cr | Highest Omega-3 potency (1271mg EPA+DHA) | Small scale; no Mg/Gut products; unprofitable |
| **YourHappyLife** | $910K | ₹0–10 Cr | Niche collagen specialization | Severely undercapitalized; single-category |
| **Be Wellness** | Bootstrapped | Sub-₹5 Cr | Pharmacist co-founder credibility | ₹5L capital; brand unknown; 2 years old |

### The Whitespace: "High-Potency + Radical Transparency"

> **None of the 6 brands simultaneously own BOTH high elemental potency AND radical transparency across all three core categories (Omega-3, Magnesium, Gut Health).**

- HK Vitals has scale but low potency
- Wellbeing Nutrition has innovation but format limits dosing
- Earthful has clean-label trust but natural sourcing limits potency
- Ace Blend has high Omega-3 potency but no Magnesium or Gut products
- YourHappyLife is collagen-only
- Be Wellness is too early-stage to assess

### Staple Wellness Positioning

**Own the territory of:**
- **Omega-3:** rTG form, 1000mg+ elemental EPA+DHA (vs. HK Vitals' 300mg Ethyl Ester)
- **Magnesium:** Pure Bisglycinate Chelate, 300mg+ elemental (vs. Earthful's likely Oxide; WN's strip-limited dosing)
- **Gut Health:** 50B+ CFU spore-forming synbiotic (vs. WN's 10B CFU Melts)

**Channel Sequence:** D2C + Amazon (Day 1) → Flipkart + Influencers (Day 90) → Q-Commerce (Day 180) → Offline Pilot (Day 365)
